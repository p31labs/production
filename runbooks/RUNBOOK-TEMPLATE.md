# RUNBOOK-TEMPLATE

> When a session resolves a repeatable procedure, the last step is writing the
> runbook. Not "next time." This template has exactly six sections.

## When to use
The trigger condition — what situation calls this procedure.

## Prerequisites
What must be true before starting.

## Steps
Numbered, deterministic where possible. Paste commands, not intent.

## How to verify
The exact command and its expected output.

## Common pitfalls
Every mistake made once becomes a warning here. Cite the actual incident.

## Owner + last verified
`Owner: <name or team>` · `Last verified: <date>`
A runbook with no owner is furniture. An owner with no review date is furniture too.
