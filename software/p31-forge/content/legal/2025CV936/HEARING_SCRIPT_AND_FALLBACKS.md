## 4. Fallbacks — procedure and pacing

| Situation | Script |
|-----------|--------|
| **Compound question** | *Your Honor, may I answer that in two short parts?* |
| **Mind goes blank** | *Your Honor, one moment — I want to answer accurately. May I refer to my notes on that single point?* |
| **Judge talks fast / you missed a word** | *Your Honor, may I have that last question repeated once? I want to answer what you actually asked.* |
| **Counsel interrupts you** | *(Look at the judge, not counsel.)* *Your Honor, I will yield — I was not finished with my answer to the Court’s question if the Court wants me to continue.* |
| **Judge cuts you off** | *Understood, Your Honor. In one sentence: [insert theme or one fact].* |
| **You started wrong lane** | *Your Honor, let me correct course — my answer to **that** issue is: …* |
| **Need a second to regulate** | *Your Honor, may I have a brief pause to organize my response?* |
| **Asked to speak to counsel** | *I will meet and confer in good faith by email as the Court directs; on the record I address the Court.* |
