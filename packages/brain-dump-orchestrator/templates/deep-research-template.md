## 3. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

Execute the following steps **in order**. For each step, explicitly state what you found and how it informs the next step.

### Step 1: Baseline Landscape
- Identify the **5‑10 most important entities** (organizations, people, regulations, technologies) in this domain.
- Map their **relationships** (competitor, partner, regulator, funder).
- Provide a **one‑paragraph overview** of the current state of the field.

### Step 2: Deep Dive – Key Dimensions
For each of the following dimensions, produce a **standalone section** with findings, evidence, and implications:

| Dimension | Guiding Questions |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Legal / Regulatory** | What laws, court rulings, or agency guidance apply? Are there pending changes? |
| **Market / Economics** | What is the total addressable market? Growth rate? Key funding sources? |
| **Technology / Infrastructure** | What platforms, standards, or architectures are dominant? What are the gaps? |
| **Competitive Landscape** | Who are the top 3‑5 players? What are their strengths/weaknesses? What is the **P31 Labs** position? |
| **Risks & Opportunities** | What are the top 3 risks? Top 3 opportunities? |

### Step 3: Synthesis & Strategic Implications
- **What are the 3 most important takeaways?**
- **What are the 2 biggest unknowns / open questions?**
- **What is a concrete, actionable recommendation for the decision context?**

### Step 4: Verification & Confidence
- For each major claim, provide a **confidence score** (High / Medium / Low) and cite the **specific source** that supports it.
- Flag any **contradictory evidence** and explain how you resolved it.
- Identify any **critical gaps** where additional primary research is needed.

## 5. CONSTRAINTS & STYLE

| Constraint | Requirement |
|------------|-------------|
| **Tone** | Professional, evidence‑driven, neutral. Avoid hype, marketing language, or advocacy. |
| **Depth** | **Do not** produce a superficial summary. This report should be actionable for a decision‑maker with domain expertise. |
| **Citations** | Every factual claim **must** have a citation. If a claim cannot be verified, explicitly state that it is unverified and why. |
| **Format** | Use headings, bullet points, and tables for clarity. The final report should be **5–10 pages** (depending on topic complexity). |
| **Iteration** | If you encounter ambiguity, state your assumption and proceed. At the end, list all assumptions made. |
