# P31 Copy Guidelines — PERFECT Version

## Core Principle

**Stop sounding like a tech startup and start sounding like a trusted tool.**

Remove all ideological/scary language. Replace with practical warmth. The magic is in the utility, not the philosophy.

---

## NEVER SAY

| Word/Phrase | Why | Alternative |
|-------------|-----|-------------|
| "Sovereign" | Triggers political anxiety. Sounds like you're taking sides in culture wars. | "Independent," "Private," "Yours to keep" |
| "Quantum" | Meaningless buzzword. Makes you sound like you're overselling. | (Just remove it — you're not doing quantum computing) |
| "Post-quantum cryptography" | Same issue. Nobody cares about the crypto tech; they care that their data is safe. | "Future-proof security" or just "Secure" |
| "Spoon-aware" | Too inside-joke for general audience. Confuses people who don't know spoon theory. | "Respects your energy levels," "Scales with your capacity" |
| "AI-native" | Meaningless. Sounds like you're chasing hype. | "AI-assisted," "Machine learning helps," or nothing |
| "Decentralized mesh network" | Too technical. Sounds like libertarian fantasy. | "Works without internet," "Offline-first" |
| "Blockchain" | (Just don't.) | (We're not using blockchain.) |
| "Biohacking" | Ableist-adjacent. Also wrong. | (Don't use this) |
| "Overcome your disability" | Patronizing. Implies the problem is you, not accessibility. | "Designed for how your brain works," "Works with you, not against you" |

## Voice & Tone

### Warm ≠ Cutesy

✅ **Good:** "We get it. You need something that actually works."
❌ **Bad:** "Let's sprinkle neurodiversity magic! 🌈✨"

### Confident ≠ Overselling

✅ **Good:** "BONDING is a multiplayer chemistry game. It works offline. Your kid can play with you, from different devices."
❌ **Bad:** "BONDING is a revolutionary neurotech application that leverages quantum molecular physics..."

### Accessible ≠ Dumbed-Down

✅ **Good:** "Every notification respects your cognitive load."
❌ **Bad:** "We use big smart computer words to make stuff simple!"

### Directness is a Feature

✅ **Good:** "We're not making money off your data. We're a nonprofit. That's the entire business model."
❌ **Bad:** "Our innovative privacy-first revenue model leverages community stewardship..."

## Copy Checklist

Before publishing **any** copy, ask:

- [ ] Does this sound like something a human would say to a friend?
- [ ] Would a 14-year-old understand it without Googling?
- [ ] Did I use any of the NEVER SAY words?
- [ ] Am I solving a real problem, or just sounding smart?
- [ ] Would a skeptical parent trust this?
- [ ] If I remove 50% of the words, does it still mean the same thing?

## Section-by-Section Rewrites

### Hero Section

**THEME:** "This is for you, and we built it with you in mind."

**NOT:**
- "Quantum-powered sovereignty"
- "Post-scarcity assistive tech"
- "Transcendent neurodiversity"

**YES:**
- "Built by neurodivergent humans, for neurodivergent humans."
- "Works with your brain, not against it."
- "Free. Open. Private. Yours."

### Features Section

**THEME:** "Specific tools for specific moments."

NOT: "Holistic neurotech ecosystem leveraging synergistic cognitive prosthetics"

YES:
- "BONDING: Multiplayer chemistry game you can play with your kids"
- "Smart Notifications: Medication reminders, break alerts, social cues"
- "The Buffer: Helps you write what you actually mean to say"

### Trust/Values Section

**THEME:** "We're not hiding anything. Here's why."

NOT: "Decentralized ledger-based privacy architecture"

YES:
- "100% Open Source. Read the code. Audit us."
- "No Ads. No Algorithms. No Tracking."
- "Made by People Who Live This. We Get It."

### Call to Action

**THEME:** "Join us. It's simple."

NOT: "Participate in the post-quantum autonomous collective"

YES:
- "Get started for free"
- "Join our Discord"
- "View the research"
- "Support our work"

## Platform-Specific Guidance

### Website Copy

- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Active voice ("We build tools" vs "Tools are built")
- Question first, answer second ("Do you need something different? We do too.")
- No marketing speak

### Research/Academic

- Technical but jargon-light
- Link to papers for depth (don't explain everything in copy)
- Focus on methodology, not ideology

### Discord/Community

- Conversational, not corporate
- Admit when you don't know
- "We're figuring this out together" energy
- Encourage questions

### Fundraising/Grants

- Impact-focused ("X families now have access to")
- Evidence-based ("Research shows")
- Humble ("We've learned")
- Clear ask ("We need $X to")

## Things We Believe (But Don't Need to Say Every Time)

- Neurodivergent people deserve tech built for them
- Privacy is a right, not a privilege
- Open source is stronger than closed source
- Data is dangerous in the wrong hands
- Accessibility benefits everyone
- Profit and mission can't coexist indefinitely

**Important:** You can *demonstrate* these beliefs through design, code, and actions. You don't need to *state* them in every headline.

---

## Red Flags (Copy That Needs a Rewrite)

- Overuse of adjectives ("revolutionary," "groundbreaking," "stunning")
- Metaphors that don't land ("moonshot," "paradigm shift," "disrupt")
- Defensive tone ("We're not Big Tech because...")
- Oversimplification ("If you just...")
- Jargon without explanation
- Promises you can't keep ("Guaranteed to solve...")

---

## The Three-Sentence Rule

If you can't explain it in three sentences, you don't understand it yet.

**Example:**

1. "P31 Labs builds free, open-source assistive technology."
2. "We focus on tools for neurodivergent families: BONDING (multiplayer game), The Buffer (communication help), and offline-first apps."
3. "We're a nonprofit. No ads. No tracking. Your privacy is the entire model."

Done. Everything else is details.

## Remember

**Your audience is:**
- Busy parents who've tried 100 things that didn't work
- Neurodivergent people who are tired of tech designed for neurotypical people
- Researchers who care about evidence, not hype
- Community members who are skeptical of tech startups

**They don't care about:**
- Your ideology
- Trendy terminology
- How smart you are
- How many buzzwords you can fit in a sentence

**They care about:**
- Does it work?
- Will you sell my data?
- Can I use it if I'm offline?
- Why should I trust you?
- Can I actually afford this?

Answer those questions. Do it clearly. Do it warmly. That's perfect.
