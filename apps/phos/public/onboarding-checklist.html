K₄ Setup — First 10 Minutes

No tech background needed. Just follow these steps.

⏱️ Before You Start

You need both people in the same room (or on a video call), each with a phone or laptop. Takes about 10 minutes.

1 Open PHOS

Go to phos.p31ca.org on your phone or laptop. It should look like a dark, quiet interface.

Click the ⚮ Attest icon in the left sidebar (looks like two intersecting rings).

2 Create Your Identity

In the Attest screen, you'll see fields for Your DID and Your Signing Key.

  • Click Generate — it creates a digital identity unique to you
  • ⚠️ Copy your Signing Key somewhere safe (Notes app, password manager, or paper)
  • Your DID (the long string starting with "did:key:") is safe to share

3 Both Connect

Now both people have their DID and Signing Key.

  • Paste your DID and your Signing Key into the top two fields
  • Paste their DID into "Peer DID"
  • Paste their Signing Key into "Peer Signing Key"

You can do this on the same device or two different devices.

4 Create the Connection

  • Leave Edge Type as "Co-Parent" (or pick "Kinship")
  • The terms box already has a simple agreement — no need to change it
  • Click the big Create Attestation button

If it works, you'll see a green box with "Attestation Created" and a long ID string.

5 Send a Message

Click the ◈ Sanctuary icon in the left sidebar.

  • Enter your DID + Signing Key in the first two fields
  • Enter their DID in the "Peer DID" field
  • Type a message and hit Send

You'll see "● Connected" in green. Messages arrive instantly.

🎁 After 24 Hours

After your first message, a 24-hour timer starts. When it finishes, you'll see a "LOVE Token Earned" box appear in Sanctuary.

Click Claim with Taler Wallet to receive it — it's a privacy-preserving digital reward for successful communication.
