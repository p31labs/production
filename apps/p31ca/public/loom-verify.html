The Loom — verify it yourself

The Loom is a hash-chained governance log: Lumi proposes, a human decides, and every step is a record anyone can check — no account, no token, no database access needed.

How a decision maps into the chain

Every action — an agent's proposal or a human's decision — is written as a record with a seq, a timestamp, and a prev_hash linking it to the record before it. Each record's own SHA-256 anchor hash commits to its predecessor, so rewriting, reordering, or deleting any row breaks the chain at a specific seq — and the verdict names it.
