The distinction is structural, not a marketing claim. P31 runs on the same Cloudflare edge as a SaaS company, but the design returns sovereignty to the family: did:key on-device, zero-telemetry by default, and a public verify endpoint anyone can check. It is a general wellness and communication platform within pre-market, non-clinical parameters.
