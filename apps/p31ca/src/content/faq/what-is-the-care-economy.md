q: What is the care economy / love ledger?
a: A way for care work to be recognized and exchanged — effort that markets don't price. P31's research frames it as the L.O.V.E. ledger (Ledger of Ontological Volume and Entropy), and the paper series develops it across care and value-form.
category: stack
---

The love ledger, the value-form problem, and the care economy are covered across the 22 DOI-verified papers in the P31 series (Zenodo, open-access). The idea is that care work — the kind a family does for each other — deserves a record, not just a market value.
