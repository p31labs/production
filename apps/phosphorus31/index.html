Our Mission

Sovereign technology for every mind.

We believe neurodivergent families deserve tools that respect their privacy, adapt to their cognition, and never lock them into a walled garden. Phosphorus 31 builds open-source, agent-native software that runs on your devices, under your control — with no data collection, no ads, and no gatekeepers.
