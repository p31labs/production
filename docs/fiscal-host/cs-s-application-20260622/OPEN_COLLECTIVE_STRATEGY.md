# P31 Labs — Open Collective Fiscal Host Strategy

## The Decision

**Path B: Join a Fiscal Host (Recommended)**

We opened the fiscal sponsorship path while the 501(c)(3) was pending; the IRS determination letter is now **confirmed (2026-09-23)**. Open Collective remains active as a donation rail.

---

## Target Fiscal Hosts

| Fiscal Host | Type | Fee | Best For |
|-------------|------|-----|----------|
| **Code for Science & Society (CS&S)** | 501(c)(3) | Varies (apply) | Public-interest technology, open infrastructure |
| **Open Source Collective (OSC)** | 501(c)(6) | 10% | Open-source software projects |
| **Open Collective Europe (OCE)** | European equivalent | 8–10% | Global projects with charitable mission |

**Primary Target:** Code for Science & Society — they are a 501(c)(3) focused on public-interest technology and open infrastructure.

**Fallback:** Open Source Collective — a 501(c)(6) that provides fiscal hosting services for open-source projects.

---

## Application Process

### Step 1: Apply to CS&S

1. Navigate to the P31 Labs Collective page on Open Collective
2. Click **"Apply to Fiscal Host"**
3. Search for **"Code for Science & Society"**
4. Submit the application using the "Open-Source Cognitive Prosthetic" narrative

**CS&S Eligibility Criteria:** Projects must align with CS&S's mission of supporting sustainable open source, open data, open science, and open infrastructure projects.

### Step 2: Mission Narrative for Application

> "P31 Labs builds open-source assistive technology for neurodivergent populations. Our flagship project, the Jitterbug Sierpinski Orchestrator, is a cognitive prosthetic that captures overwhelming complexity, breaks it into manageable pieces, and converges on reliable answers. We are a Georgia nonprofit with 501(c)(3) status confirmed (EIN 42-1888158, IRS determination 2026-09-23), incorporated April 3, 2026. Our work aligns with CS&S's mission to make knowledge and knowledge production more accessible."

### Step 3: Connect Mercury to Stripe

Once the Fiscal Host application is approved:

1. Go to Fiscal Host Dashboard > Settings > Receiving Money
2. Click **"Connect Stripe"**
3. Stripe will guide you through connecting your Mercury bank account

## Fallback: Open Source Collective (OSC)

If CS&S does not approve the application:

1. Apply to OSC via the Open Collective platform
2. OSC is a 501(c)(6) — donations are **not** tax-deductible, but they still provide fiscal hosting services
3. This is sufficient to receive the ASAN grant funds (the grant is not tax-deductible; it's an expense)
