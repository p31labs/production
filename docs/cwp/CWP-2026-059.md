**Critical context:** The NGI Zero Commons Fund (the most comprehensive fund within NGI) closed on June 1, 2026. After August 1, 2026, these two pilot programmes will also close. **This is the last window for NGI funding.**

NLnet received over 10,000 applications over the lifetime of NGI, with over 2,500 applications submitted in just the first five months of 2026. The competition is intense. P31 must stand out.

### 1.2 EUDI Wallet: The December 2026 Mandate

By **December 2026**, every EU member state must offer at least one certified EU Digital Identity (EUDI) Wallet. Fewer than one third of member states currently meet the readiness benchmark.

**Key deadlines:**
- **November 2026:** Every EU member state must make an EUDI Wallet available to any citizen or resident who wants one
- **December 2026:** Every EU member state must offer at least one certified EUDI Wallet
- **~December 2027:** Organisations must legally accept EUDI Wallets

**P31's alignment:** The P31 Sovereign Mesh already implements SD-JWT Verifiable Credentials (draft-17), selective disclosure, revocation via Status-List-2021, and a CredentialIssuer service endpoint. **P31 is EUDI-ready.** This is a competitive differentiator.

### 1.3 Post-Quantum Cryptography: NIST Standards Are Here

In May 2026, the IETF published **RFC 9964** — ML-DSA for JOSE and COSE. NIST has released initial working drafts of proposed updates to the Personal Identity Verification (PIV) standards to support ML-DSA and ML-KEM.

**The PQC timeline:**
- **May 2026:** RFC 9964 published (ML-DSA for JOSE/COSE)
- **2026:** NIST drafts PIV updates for PQC
- **January 2027:** PQC-supported DevIDs expected to become a requirement for many government procurements

**P31's alignment:** The Genesis Gate already uses ML-DSA-65 (FIPS 204) for post-quantum co-signatures. The ledger-bridge anchors composite signatures (Ed25519 + ML-DSA-65) on-chain. **P31 is PQC-ready.** This is another competitive differentiator.

### 1.4 Decentralized Identifiers: W3C Candidate Recommendation

In March 2026, the W3C published **Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs) v1.1 as a Candidate Recommendation**. The specification covers DID syntax, a common data model, core properties, serialized representations, DID operations, and the process of resolving DIDs.

**P31's alignment:** P31 uses `did:key` (Ed25519) and `did:jwk` (ML-DSA-65) throughout the stack. The federation-bridge exposes a DID Document at `federation.p31ca.org/actor`. **P31 is DID-compliant.** This is a third competitive differentiator.

### 1.5 The Open Internet Stack: What Comes After NGI

NLnet is transitioning from NGI to the **Open Internet Stack (OIS)**, part of the European Commission's new Tech Sovereignty package. The OIS will continue funding open internet technologies, but the application process will be restructured.

**Strategic implication:** P31 should **submit to NGI TALER and Fediversity now**, before the August 1 deadline. After that, the funding landscape will change. A successful NGI grant provides credibility for future OIS applications.
