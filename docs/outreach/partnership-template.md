**What P31 is:**

P31 is a sovereign, neuroinclusive platform that lets families build their own tools — no code required. A parent can describe what they want in natural language (e.g., "a star-catching game for my 6-year-old") and P31 generates a working, accessible, deployable app. Every app is private, offline-capable, and respects the user's cognitive energy level (spoon-aware).

**What we've built:**

- **The Calcium Cage** — a 9-level progressive onboarding game that teaches phosphorus chemistry, 863 Hz physics, K₄ topology, and sovereign identity through interactive discovery
- **WILLOW** — a child companion app with voice messages, mood tracking, drawing, and family contacts (named after my daughter, who turns 7 on August 8)
- **BONDING** — a multiplayer chemistry game that lets parents play alongside their children from separate devices (shipped on my son's 10th birthday, 488 tests passing)
- **Vibe Studio** — a visual AI code generator with multi-agent pipeline and sandboxed preview
- **22 peer-reviewed papers** on Zenodo (ORCID 0009-0002-2492-9079)
