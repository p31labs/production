# Spoon Check-In — Start / Mid / End of Session

**Read aloud:**
"Before we start, a quick energy check. On a scale of 1 to 5 — 1 is 'very low, need to rest,' 5 is 'full energy, ready to go' — where are you right now?"

**Record start:** _____

- **1–2:** "Thanks for being here. We'll keep this short and can break anytime. Rescheduling is totally fine too."
- **3:** "Great — we'll aim for [shorter length] and check in midway."
- **4–5:** "Wonderful, let's dive in — but we'll still check in midway."

**Mid-session:** "How's your energy now — same, higher, or lower?" Record: _____
(if dropped ≥2 → offer break or shorten remaining tasks)

**End:** "And now, same 1–5 scale?" Record: _____
