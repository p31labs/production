## PEREZ V. STURGIS PUBLIC SCHOOLS (2023)

**Citation:** 598 U.S. ___ (2023)
**Holding:** IDEA's exhaustion requirement does not preclude ADA lawsuits for compensatory damages because the relief sought is not something IDEA can provide.
**Impact:** Families can now sue districts directly for monetary damages without exhausting administrative remedies. Districts face $50k-$150k in uninsured legal liability per unresolved accommodation gap.

---

## ADA TITLE II (42 U.S.C. § 12132)

"No qualified individual with a disability shall, by reason of such disability, be excluded from participation in or be denied the benefits of the services, programs, or activities of a public entity."

**Impact:** School districts are public entities subject to ADA Title II. Failure to provide reasonable accommodations can result in direct monetary liability.

---

## FERPA (20 U.S.C. § 1232g)

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act protects the privacy of student education records. The U.S. Department of Education actively enforces FERPA compliance.

**Impact:** School districts must maintain chain of custody for student data. Use of public LLMs with student PII breaks FERPA chain of custody.

---

## FRE 902(13)

Federal Rule of Evidence 902(13) covers "Certified Records Generated by an Electronic Process or System" — self-authenticating if accompanied by proper technical certification.

**Impact:** P31 Andromeda's compliance ledgers are admissible in court without expert testimony.

---

## IDEA PART B SECTION 611 (20 U.S.C. § 1411)

Funds may be used for assistive technology, extended school year services, personnel training, and parent training.

**Impact:** Direct procurement pathway for P31 Andromeda without board vote.

---

## GEORGIA ANNUAL REGISTRATION

Georgia domestic nonprofit corporations must file annual registration with the Secretary of State by July 2 each year.

**Impact:** P31 Labs, Inc. must file by July 2, 2026.
