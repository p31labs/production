# The Loom — Pricing Model (family-first)

**Status:** 2026-09-23. Distinct from the Andromeda/IEP pricing
(`docs/launch/pricing-model.md`). ⚠️ pricing is a financial decision — human
reviews before use.

## Principles

- **Grants are the primary revenue channel** for a nonprofit; the product is
  open-source core + a paid managed tier, not a SaaS toll.
- **Family-first means affordable-to-zero for a family**, sustainable via
  grants/donations, with a paid path only where institutions or large
  households derive commercial value.
- Open-source core (Apache-2.0/MIT) is a **funding advantage**, not a
  concession — Humanity AI prioritizes open-weight/open-source.

## Tiers

| Tier | Price | Who | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Community (self-host)** | $0 | individual families, contributors | the full open-source core: chain, gate, codename system, self-deploy. No SLA. |
| **Family Workspace (managed)** | ⚠️ propose $0–$48/yr (donation tier) | a family | hosted Loom on loom.p31ca.org, one family log, personal/shared scope, live `/verify`. Funded by donations; the simplified donation form is the revenue lever. |
| **Household+ / Enterprise** | ⚠️ propose contact-sales | large households, schools, clinics, institutions | multi-family/multi-tenant administration, SLA, data-residency options, compliance export (EU AI Act Art 12 evidence). |
| **Custom (sovereign integration)** | $2,500–4,500 fixed per integration (align with `INCOME-SME.md` rate card) | orgs needing governance embedded | guardAgentEvent into an existing agent stack; PQC capability tokens; refusal sidecar. |

## Notes / honest limits

- Zero revenue today. The donation tier must convert; the +52% form-simplification
  lever applies (see `docs/PRODUCTION-READINESS.md`).
- The concierge/contract income lane ($125/hr, per `INCOME-SME.md`) is the
  near-term survival revenue; pricing above is the product lane.
- Do not publish a price until the family session validates the offer; the
  session answers "would you pay for this?" ⚠️ — not yet asked.
