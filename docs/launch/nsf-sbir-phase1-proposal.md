## PROJECT SUMMARY (1/2 page)

P31 Andromeda is a zero-server, local-first platform that generates tamper-evident proof of IEP (Individualized Education Program) accommodation delivery. Every IEP interaction is timestamped, encrypted, and logged, producing sealed PDF compliance ledgers that are **FRE 902(13) self-authenticating** — admissible in court without expert testimony.

Following *Perez v. Sturgis Public Schools* (598 U.S. ___, 2023), families can now sue school districts directly for ADA monetary damages without exhausting IDEA administrative remedies. Districts face $50K–$150K in uninsured legal liability per unresolved accommodation gap. Simultaneously, 70% of SPED educators enter raw student PII into public LLMs, breaking FERPA chain-of-custody.

P31 Andromeda eliminates both risks: zero cloud servers means zero data breach risk, and every compliance event is cryptographically logged. The platform runs on cheap donated hardware ($35 Chromebooks), requires no broadband, and is open-source (MIT/Apache 2.0 core, commercial enterprise license).

## PHASE I OBJECTIVES

1. **Deploy pilot with 3–5 school districts.** collect baseline compliance data.
2. **Optimize FRE 902(13) ledger generation.** Validate admissible output against court standards.
3. **Measure ROI:** Compare pilot districts' legal exposure pre/post deployment.
4. **Publish open-source core.** Establish developer community and documentation.

## INNOVATION

Unlike cloud-based IEP platforms, P31 Andromeda is:
- **Zero-server:** No cloud data stores. Eliminates breach surface.
- **Cryptographically sealed:** Ed25519-signed entries. ML-DSA reserved for PQC migration.
- **Offline-first:** Fully functional on $35 donated hardware. No broadband required.
- **Voice-first:** Every interaction path has a voice equivalent (CogPass v4.1 dynamic personas).
- **Transparent:** Open-source core. Districts can audit every line of code.
