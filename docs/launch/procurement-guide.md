# P31 Andromeda — Procurement Guide for K-12 Districts

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

P31 Andromeda is a zero-server, local-first platform that generates tamper-evident, FRE 902(13)-self-authenticating IEP compliance ledgers. It can be procured using existing IDEA Part B Section 611 funds and micro-purchase authority — no board vote required.

---

## PROCUREMENT PATH 1: MICRO-PURCHASE (<$10,000)

- **Threshold:** Federal micro-purchase threshold is $10,000-$15,000
- **Authority:** Principal or SPED Director authorizes instantly
- **Documentation:** Price considered reasonable based on research, experience, or purchase history

**Steps:**
1. Identify need (IEP compliance documentation)
2. Evaluate product (request demo)
3. Pilot (Free Compliance Audit for 5 highest-risk IEPs)
4. Purchase: Submit requisition with price justification
5. Implementation: White-glove onboarding

---

## PROCUREMENT PATH 2: SMALL PURCHASE ($10,000-$49,999)

- **Threshold:** $10,000-$49,999
- **Authority:** Superintendent or procurement officer
- **Documentation:** Quotes from 3 suppliers (or sole-source justification)

**Steps:**
1. Identify need
2. Request quotes (or sole-source justification)
3. Evaluate
4. Pilot
5. Purchase

---

## FUNDING SOURCE: IDEA PART B SECTION 611

**Citation:** IDEA Part B Section 611 (20 U.S.C. § 1411)
**Allowable uses:** Assistive technology, extended school year services, personnel training, parent training
**Impact:** No board vote required for assistive technology purchases.

---

## PILOT PATH: FREE COMPLIANCE AUDIT

**Offer:** We audit your 5 highest-risk IEPs and generate an FRE 902(13) self-authenticating compliance ledger — free.
**Deliverable:** Tamper-evident evidence of IEP compliance.
**Outcome:** If valuable, expand to full caseload.

---

## TIMING

K-12 districts buy on July-June fiscal year with peak purchasing in March-June. Pilot can begin immediately. Full implementation typically 4-8 weeks.
