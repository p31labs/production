# P31 Andromeda — Pricing Model

## TIER 1: FREE (Community)
- **Price:** $0
- **Audience:** Individuals, open-source contributors
- **Features:** Access to all software (MIT/Apache 2.0 core), community support, self-deployment, no SLA

## TIER 2: PILOT (Single School/District)
- **Price:** $4,800-$9,900/year
- **Audience:** Single school, small district
- **Features:** White-glove onboarding, priority support, custom integrations, FRE 902(13) compliance ledger generation, 3-5 highest-risk IEPs audited, tamper-evident documentation

## TIER 3: ENTERPRISE (Multi-District/State Consortia)
- **Price:** Contact sales
- **Audience:** Multi-district, state consortia, SPED cooperatives
- **Features:** SLA, full support, training and professional development, compliance audits, custom deployments, multi-tenant administration, data residency/sovereignty options

## PROCUREMENT NOTES
- Micro-Purchase (<$10,000): Principal or SPED Director authorizes instantly
- IDEA Part B Section 611 funds explicitly allow assistive technology
- Federal micro-purchase threshold: $10,000-$15,000
- K-12 districts buy on July-June fiscal year with peak purchasing March-June
