## Why this document exists

This is not a marketing language and it is not an invention. It is a *naming of
language that is already in the work* — the electrical and structural metaphors
the operator has used consistently across the architecture, the papers, and the
public surfaces. The lexicon makes that vocabulary explicit so it is used the
same way every time, and so the "AI-trigger word" problem has a native
alternative.

## The two axes (read this before anything else)

The GENAI policy and the combined-cognition framing are not in conflict because
they answer different questions:

- **"Tool, never collaborator"** answers: *who is the author, and who holds
  accountability?* → **the human.** This never changes.
- **"Combined cognition"** answers: *what kind of system produces the output?*
  → **one integrated system, two substrates.**

A carpenter is the author of the table. The table is also produced by a system
of carpenter, saw, jig, and material. Both statements are true; neither
contradicts the other. The mistake the industry makes is conflating the two
questions. This lexicon exists to keep them separate.

**The authorship line is held constant in every register.** "Combined
cognition" describes production. It never moves authorship.

## The lexicon

### combined cognition
**Definition.** One system, two substrates — the human and the computational
instrument functioning as a single cognitive unit. Not "a human using a tool"
(implies separation), not "human-AI collaboration" (implies two agents in
dialogue). One thing.

**Use.** The umbrella term for the production system. Never a synonym for
"the AI" — there is no "the AI," there is the system.

**The electrical reading.** The operator's framing: *"the human is the grounded
neutral between the machine's ungrounded delta and consequence."* The system is
wye-connected — grounded at the human — while the machine alone is delta —
ungrounded, and dangerous to touch.

### the fuse
**Definition.** The human. A fuse is a deliberate weak point that protects the
whole circuit; it is the point where excess current is interrupted. The human
is the accountability point — the component that breaks before the system
does, so the rest survives.

**Use.** Describes the operator's role in the system. *"The human is the fuse
— the point of accountability between the machine's output and consequence."*

### the bus bar
**Definition.** The system itself. A bus bar is the conductive bar everything
connects to; it carries the combined current. The operator has used this term
consistently for the Loom and the infrastructure.

**Use.** The system is not a tool or a partner. It is the infrastructure
through which combined cognition flows. *"The Loom is the bus bar."*

### the tie bus
**Definition.** The interface where the two substrates connect. Not a chat
window. A bus bar — the point where the human's intentions and the
instrument's responses tie together.

**Use.** Describes the actual interface (the session, the prompt surface, the
gate). The tie bus is where a single intent becomes a combined response.

### wye-delta shift
**Definition.** The transformation from ungrounded machine cognition (delta,
open triangle, no neutral) to grounded combined cognition (wye, star, with a
neutral point). The human provides the neutral point that makes the system
safe to operate.

**Use.** Describes what happens when human grounding is added to raw machine
output. *"The system is the wye-to-delta shift made durable."*

### provenance, not confession
**Definition.** The disclosure frame. Process transparency (how the work was
made, what was verified, what the audit chain shows) instead of source
disclosure (whether "AI" was involved). The reproducible verify command is the
model — it demonstrates, it does not confess.

**Use.** In every public surface, the trust anchor is the verifiable process,
never a statement about the author's identity. *"Provenance, not confession."*

### the system
**Definition.** The unified voice. When the work speaks, it speaks as one
thing. Never "I used AI," never "the AI and I." Just: the system.

**Use.** The public register's first-person-plural-free voice. The work is
described by what it does and how it is verified — not by how it was produced.

## What this replaces

| Phrase | Replaced by | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "AI-assisted" (in Register-1 public narrative) | the system / provenance-not-confession | "AI" is a trigger word that reduces trust in true content (CISPA 2026) |
| "human-AI collaboration" | combined cognition | implies two agents in dialogue; the system is one thing |
| "an AI as my only tool" (in Register 3 / policy) | still accurate — the human is the author | the policy line is about authorship, kept intact |
| "the AI and I" | the system | the unified voice |

The term "AI-assisted" remains accurate and REQUIRED in Register 3 (the
GENAI policy, the provenance log, grant disclosures) — where the audience
needs the production truth. The lexicon governs Register 1 (public) and
Register 2 (professional), where the production story is told at the level
each audience needs.
