## What this session is

A ~90-minute first-use session on the Loom, a shared agent/human event log. The
concierge reads one proposal as Lumi would propose it ("Lumi suggests X. Yes or
no?"), the participant answers, and both sides are recorded as real events in
the Loom's append-only, hash-chained audit log. The session ends with a short
survey (CSAT 1–5, NPS 0–10, "what would you change?").

## What is recorded

1. **Log events** — the proposal and the decision, stored in the hash-chained
   audit trail. Events carry a code name (`statedBy`), never a legal name, age,
   gender, or neurotype.
2. **A 3-minute video** — proposal → pause → decision — used in funding
   applications (NLnet, Humanity AI, Sentient) and the public demo.
3. **A verbatim capture sheet** — your exact words at the decision moment,
   written into `docs/concierge-session-1.md`.

## Your rights

- **You can decline the video and still participate.** Declining the video
  release does not end the session.
- **You can withdraw consent at any time.** If you withdraw, the session stops;
  nothing is deleted from the chain (it is append-only by design) — an honest
  annotation is written instead.
- **You may inspect or approve the finished recording** before it is used.
- Data is stored securely and destroyed no later than 5 years after publication
  or presentation of findings.
