# P31 Story for Funders — Master 1-Pager

## The Human Problem

Modern assistive technology has abandoned the people it was built to protect. For neurodivergent families managing profound executive dysfunction and systemic isolation, current tools present a toxic choice: rely on expensive, brittle SaaS platforms that gatekeep accessibility, or submit to predatory ecosystems that monetize intimate family communications through data-harvesting.

## The Sovereign Solution

P31 Labs, Inc. — a Georgia Domestic Nonprofit corporation (incorporated May 4, 2026) with 501(c)(3) status confirmed (IRS determination letter received 2026-09-23, EIN 42-1888158) — rejects this paradigm. We design, build, and deploy an open-source, non-custodial, surveillance-free "Sovereign Stack." This infrastructure returns digital autonomy to families as a basic civil right. It operates strictly as a general wellness and communication support platform within pre-market, non-clinical parameters, ensuring cognitive prosthetics remain functional even if cloud connectivity is severed.

## Lived-Experience Grounding

P31 Labs is driven by direct execution, not academic abstraction. Founded by Will Johnson, an AuDHD engineer and former DoD civilian. As a single father to two neurodivergent children (S.J. and W.J.), the stack was forged out of immediate household survival during severe medical and legal hardship. Every line of code is stress-tested in a live, multi-adversarial environment.
