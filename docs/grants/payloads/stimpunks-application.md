Stimpunks exists to help disabled and neurodivergent people build community, share skills, and create mutual aid. PHOS Mesh is infrastructure for exactly that — a communication and coordination platform built by a disabled engineer, for disabled communities, with privacy and accessibility as core architectural principles.

## The Problem

Disabled people are disproportionately isolated. We are also disproportionately surveilled — by the services we depend on, by the institutions that manage our benefits, and by the platforms we use to stay connected. The "free" services extract data. The accessible services are closed-source. The privacy-respecting services require technical expertise we may not have, especially on bad brain days.

## The Solution: PHOS Mesh

PHOS Mesh is the family/community networking layer of the PHOS cognitive operating system. It provides:

- **Offline-first mesh communication:** No internet required. Messages hop between devices via WebRTC P2P and (in the hardware variant) LoRa radio.
- **Spoon-aware presence:** Each user's cognitive energy level is visible to trusted contacts — not as a number, but as a color and a simple message.
- **Crisis protocol integration:** When a user hits 0 spoons, the Guardian activates. All local stimulation ceases. A silent notification goes to the trusted mesh.
- **Zero data extraction:** No cloud. No accounts. No analytics. All state lives on-device.

## What We Will Do

With $5,000 from Stimpunks, we will:

1. **Expand mesh from family to community** — support community groups of 20+ nodes with moderation tools.
2. **Add mutual aid coordination** — a "needs board" surface for requests and offers, visible only to mesh members.
3. **Hardware prototype for offline mesh** — ESP32-S3 + SX1262 LoRa pocket nodes (~$35 each).
4. **Documentation for non-technical users** — "PHOS Mesh for Mutual Aid Groups" guide.
