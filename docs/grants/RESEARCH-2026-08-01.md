## 7. NLnet evaluation process — what to expect

Source: nlnet.nl/commonsfund/guideforapplicants + NLnet FAQ.

- Each proposal is reviewed independently by **at least two review committee members**.
- Budgets are assessed for **frugality** and reasonableness against the work plan.
- **Failed proposals receive the reviewers' concerns** and are pushed back to the
  next call rather than silently discarded.
- Decision window: typically **4–8 weeks after the deadline** (mid-September to
  mid-October 2026 for this call).
- Contact: NLnet will email the applicant within a few days of the deadline;
  check spam + any legacy mail forwarding (DMARC/DKIM/SPF drops).

## 8. Open Internet Stack transition — what it means for P31

Source: nlnet.nl/news/2026/20260612-NGIZero-stocktaking.html (published 2026-08-01).

- NGI Zero is in a **concluding phase**; NLnet is "taking stock of a decade of NGI Zero".
- After summer, **three new programmes under the Open Internet Stack umbrella** will be announced.
- Until then, NLnet **only accepts proposals for NGI TALER and NGI Fediversity**;
  the regular open call reopens after summer.
- Statistics: 10,000+ applications, 1,400 projects over 10 years.
- **Implication:** parked P31 Portal supplement (€5K) and the archived
  K4-Mesh-Core (€35K / €15K variants) should be retargeted to the Open Internet
  Stack programmes once they are announced (see OPEN-INTERNET-STACK-RETARGET.md).
