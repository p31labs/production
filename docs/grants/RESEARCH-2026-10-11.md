## 1. Humanity AI — Communities Leading on AI · LIVE · $10M

- **Deadline:** 2026-10-21, 4:59 PM PT / 7:59 PM ET. Source: `humanityai.ai/open-call`.
- **Pooled initiative** of 10 philanthropies: Ford, MacArthur, Mellon, Mozilla,
  Omidyar Network, Doris Duke, Lumina, Kapor, Packard, Siegel Family Endowment.
- **Amount:** $10M open call (grants $75K–$1M, up to 3 years). *Do not cite the
  $18M figure — that was the May 2026 initial grant round, not this call.*
- **Eligibility:** US-based 501(c)(3) lead applicant. Four application paths:
  independent 501(c)(3), 501(c)(3) leading a collaboration, fiscal sponsor,
  Tribal government. For-profits may participate but cannot lead.
- **Issue areas:** Education · Labor & economy · Humanities/arts/culture.
  **Approaches:** (1) shaping AI governance, (2) building AI on community terms,
  (3) challenging AI that doesn't serve communities.
- **Exclusions:** AI literacy, AI product adoption, pure research. Priority given
  to open-weight / open-source models.
- **Strategic asset:** the portal offers to share select applicant data with
  interested funders *beyond* the call (explicit consent). Consent = YES is a
  discovery channel into all ten backers.
- **P31 fit:** community governance of care AI (11 gates + provenance ledger +
  pickle-name identity). "Audit approaches that ensure AI systems work as
  intended" is a named funder priority.

## 2. OTF Internet Freedom Fund · ROLLING · ⚠️ POLITICALLY COMPROMISED

- **Process:** rolling; Stage 1 = concept note, Stage 2 = proposal by invitation.
  6–8 week determination. $10K–$900K, target ceiling ~$300K, ideal $50K–$200K /
  6–12 mo.
- **⚠️ Court won, under appeal:** D.D.C. (Judge Royce C. Lamberth) **granted OTF
  summary judgment 2026-03-26**, denying USAGM's motions and ordering release of
  the withheld FY2025 funds; OTF states all $43.5M FY2024 funds are disbursed.
  **The administration has appealed**, and OTF's own filings state it "faces
  imminent closure" if the termination is upheld. Long-time chief Libby Liu
  departed. `CourtListener #70, 1:25-cv-00840`; `netzpolitik.org`; `Der Standard`.
- **Implication:** file the concept note (low-effort, one of several shots), but
  do **not** build a bridge loan or the "first check" plan against an OTF award
  timeline. Humanity AI is the higher-probability check.

## 3. OTF FOSS Sustainability Fund · NOT YET ELIGIBLE · 2027

- Eligibility: software released **3+ years**, active development, updates 4+/yr,
  active coding in last 2 years, substantial user base in repressive contexts.
- Awards $150K–$400K / ~24 mo, ~4 projects per cycle. 2026 cycle closed May 7.
  Watch for 2027 spring cycle. P31 does not qualify yet.

## 4. NLnet Restack (Open Internet Stack) · DEADLINE 2026-11-03

- **Deadline:** 2026-11-03, 12:00 noon CET (first call opened 2026-09-03).
  Cadence shifted: even→odd months, 1st→3rd day.
- **Amount:** €5K–50K first grant; >€50K (max €150K) requires a prior concluded
  project. Scoring 30% technical merit / 40% strategic relevance / 30% value for
  money. Anyone may apply (incl. 501(c)(3)).
- **⚠️ GenAI policy = v1.1 in force (2026-01-26), no revision published.** It
  excludes "vibecoding" (mostly LLM-generated work) and **requires disclosure +
  a prompt-provenance log** (model, dates/times, prompts, unedited output).
  **Action:** do not wait for a revision that may not arrive before Nov 3; submit
  under v1.1 with maximum disclosure and attach the prompt-provenance log.
  `nlnet.nl/foundation/policies/generativeAI/`.

## 5. NIST SBIR/STTR · PARTNER ROUTE · REAUTHORIZED THROUGH 2031

- Both chambers authorized SBIR/STTR through **2031-09-30** (Small Business
  Innovation and Economic Security Act). NIST reports **no active NOFO**; next
  deadline TBD. Phase I ≤$100K, Phase II ≤$400K. Quantum information science,
  cybersecurity, AI are in scope.
- **PQC topic caveat:** `sbir.gov` lists a "Cryptography, including Post-quantum
  Cryptography" topic with cycle deadlines **2026-11-04, 2027-03-04, 2027-07-07**
  (agency TBD — verify the topic's sponsoring agency before pitching). This is
  the origin of the old "2026-05-22 → 2027-07-07" string; it is a real topic
  cycle range, not a program-wide window.
- **Eligibility:** for-profit SBC prime only; nonprofits = subcontractor or STTR
  partner (RI must perform 30–60% of research). P31 cannot be prime.
- **Action:** Q2 2027 play with a for-profit partner. Monitor nist.gov monthly.

## 6. Sloan Foundation — Digital Technology · ROLLING LOI · UP TO $1.25M

- Rolling Letter of Inquiry (2 pages max) to Program Director Joshua M.
  Greenberg (`greenberg@sloan.org`). Exploratory grants commonly $50K–$400K, but
  the Technology program ceiling reaches **$1,250,000**. Major grants (>$250K)
  approved quarterly.
- **Fit:** PQC primitives + sovereign identity as "open, reusable tools."
  Technical, infrastructure-framed — *not* the community narrative (that's
  Humanity AI's lane).

## 7. Gates Foundation — Digital Public Infrastructure · RELATIONSHIP

- **Civitas ID precedent:** San Francisco nonprofit received a **$500K / 2-year**
  Gates grant (Aug 2026) for open-source digital identity for people excluded
  from formal systems — nearly P31's sovereign-identity asset verbatim. Other DPI
  grants: IPA $868,612 (DC), Carnegie Mellon $879,757, UNDP $2M.
- **Action:** no open LOI portal confirmed. 2027 play; warm intro via Omidyar
  (Humanity AI backer, DPI-adjacent). Start through the Humanity AI consent
  mechanism, not a cold LOI.

## 9. Ford Foundation — Digital Infrastructure Insights Fund · FINAL OPEN CALL

- D//F 2026 RFP is the fund's **final limited open call**. Short exploratory

## 10. Pivotal Ventures — Care Economy · 2027

## 11. Mozilla Foundation — Democracy x AI Cohort · CLOSED 2026

## 12. Knight Foundation — AI Ethics & Governance · NEW WATCHLIST

- **Ethics and Governance of AI Fund** (fiscal sponsor: The Miami Foundation;
  initial support Knight Foundation + others) — seeks orgs that "bolster the
  voice of civil society in shaping the development of AI in the public
  interest." Direct fit for the **agent-governance** asset (11 gates +
  provenance ledger).
- **AI Ethics and Governance Initiative** (MIT Media Lab + Harvard Berkman-Klein;
  supported by Knight + Luminate). Academic/research vehicle, not P31's lane.
- **Action:** add to the 2027 watchlist; monitor for an open call. Exploratory
  band, exact amount TBD.

## 13. Federal-grant headwind (applies to NIST SBIR, LSC TIG)

- OMB proposed a rewrite of 2 CFR Part 200 (Docket OMB-2026-0034), the framework
  governing all federal grants; if finalized it takes effect 2026-10-01 and adds
  compliance burden (Do Not Pay pre-payment checks, pass-through rules).
  Congressional appropriators urged withdrawal. Monitor before relying on any
  federal-rail timeline.
