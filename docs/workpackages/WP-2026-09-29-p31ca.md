## Anti-drift guards

- **Single-writer per fact:** A layout, B workspace, C publish, D deprecation, E provenance.
- **Shared gate set:** same convergence commands at every facet.
- **Named convergence:** specific command, specific expected output.
- **Negative control at every convergence:** a gate that cannot fail is furniture.
- **Register is authoritative:** Rekor log is external record; `.govern-audit.jsonl` is internal.
