Our Mission

Building the infrastructure for a neurodivergent-led care economy.

P31 Labs builds open-source, neurodivergent-first assistive technology because the tools we need don't exist yet — and the ones that do weren't made for us. Our name comes from the tetrahedron: four vertices, six edges, four faces — the simplest possible volume, grounding sovereign data structures, peer-to-peer care networks, and post-quantum cryptography.

We create technology that amplifies human potential, fosters genuine community connections, and builds a more equitable and accessible digital future — with no data collection, no ads, and no gatekeepers.
